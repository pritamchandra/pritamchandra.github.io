<p class="verse">And it'll remain in my memories, maybe as a wide stretch of blur
Struggling as she stands, the first time I see her.
She doesn't know which road to take, uneasy and lone,
Somehow I know I have found my stray heart a home.

In a sea of people who were selfish and rude,
As frail as a flower, helpless she stood,
And I want to rush past these people, for her hands I would take,
And I believe it is worth to put everything at stake.

But the same cruel people have thorned the way's length,
To seize me to their world against all my strength,
I crumbled; I fell, yet at her side as I came
I wrapped her in my arms as I guarded her from them.

I feel her breath at the end, her fingers clutching my shirt.
The quiet sobs of satisfaction, the pleading beats of heart!
And all that we feel had our souls shared then,
While she feels my Love, I just feel her pain.

When I open my eyes she was nowhere to be found,
And I wonder with pain, if to their world I am bound!
At last behind that crowd, fades the face of someone,
Weakly as she smiles, yet failing to stand,
And there is my love gently waving her hand.</p>

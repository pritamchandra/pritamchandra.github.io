<p class="verse">Few years back in time when marched his mind,
To a thought that now seems the strangest of its kind,
To have considered the golden park to be one childish place,
Now he observes with a smile, what childishness!

For ones accompanying their so called partners for life,
Dates or girlfriends, while few emerge with wives,
These beloveds keep smiling, while the shy men just sigh,
Shifting their gaze across the wide evening sky.

In the corner of the park where hormones don't rule,
Is swarming with teens, loud and cheerful;
There the amusing air is combined with the blends
Of cigarette smoke and their weird worldly trends.

Towards the end of the lawn, usually dark by the fence,
An exaggerated carpet hosts a round conference,
As if in the middle of some sort of serious card game,
All engaged in silence are the middle aged men.

And then the petite ones, they would just shout and sing,
As all they care about are the slide and the swing.
Swinging and sliding their preoccupied thoughts,
From bookish aftermaths to less grave plots.

Scattered all around, maybe he notices at last,
The bodies in the present, and hearts lost in past,
Mingled in memories of their youth made of gold,
Making a few more of it is the pack of the old.

In a lonely corner with a pen and curious eyes,
He was witnessing the strangeness that life implies,
And he knows that someday it might get dark,
But the moon would never wan o'er the Golden Park.</p>

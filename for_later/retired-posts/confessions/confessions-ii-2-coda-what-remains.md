<p class="verse">Not certainty. Never that.
A little more honesty than I started with,
a shorter list of things I&rsquo;m sure of,
a longer list of things I&rsquo;m willing to keep asking.

If that is not faith, call it attention.
I have been paying it anyway,
which, on the days that matter,
turned out to be the same thing.</p>

<p class="verse">You were the first shape I gave to the dark,
and I want to say I outgrew you cleanly,
the way a coat gets too small.
That isn&rsquo;t true. I cut you off
in pieces, over years,
and kept some of the pieces.

I don&rsquo;t think I was wrong to hold you once.
I think you were a room I needed
before I could build a house.
I am writing from the house now.
I left the door unlocked,
in case you wanted to see it.</p>

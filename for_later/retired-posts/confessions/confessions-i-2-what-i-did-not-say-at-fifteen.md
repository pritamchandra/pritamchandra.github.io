<p class="verse">At fifteen I knew the answers
the way you know a room in the dark &mdash;
by furniture, not by light.
I could recite the whole catechism
and mean about half of it,
which felt, at the time, like enough.

Nobody asked what I actually believed.
I wouldn&rsquo;t have known how to answer.
I have spent the years since
learning to sit in that room
with the lights on,
counting what&rsquo;s still there.</p>

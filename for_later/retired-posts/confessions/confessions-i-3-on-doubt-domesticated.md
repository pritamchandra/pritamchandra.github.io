<p class="verse">Doubt moved in quietly, the way a cat does &mdash;
uninvited, then permanent.
It does not raise its voice.
It just sits where I can see it
while I try to work.

I used to think faith meant
getting the cat to leave.
Now I think it means
learning its name,
feeding it on schedule,
letting it watch while I pray anyway.</p>

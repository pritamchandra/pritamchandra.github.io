<p class="verse">
There may be rain on the trees,
Afternoon breeze.
Friends waiting at the bay
and music to play.
A treat for the mind
in some answers to find.
But no delight to embrace
when frown&rsquo;s on her face.

There maybe grudges to speak.
Fairness to seek,
counting the right and the wrong,
all our traumas of long.
All my hurts to stress.
And the efforts to express.
All irrelevant pile
without her smile.

There maybe pain to endure,
uncertainty sure.
Some doubting of the plain
and questions in vain.
Irrational fears
or a whole winter of tears.
Yet, nothing drains more
when her spirit's sore.</p>

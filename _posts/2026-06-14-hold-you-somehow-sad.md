I wrote this one slowly, over a few weeks where nothing was especially wrong and I still couldn't shake the low hum of it &mdash; the ordinary, unglamorous sadness that doesn't have a cause you can point to, and so doesn't really invite anyone to help. I kept starting to write a song about fixing that feeling, and it kept turning into a song about sitting next to it instead. This is the second version.

I almost didn't post this one, mostly because it doesn't resolve &mdash; there's no bridge-out into hope, no key change that fixes things. But that felt more honest than the alternative, and I'd rather the blog have a few unresolved things on it than only the tidy ones.

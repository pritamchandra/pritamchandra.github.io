I am a mathematician, currently at [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/department/department-of-mathematics/), working in Matrix Analysis with [Prof. Rajendra Bhatia](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QQYGgRoAAAAJ&hl=en). Previously, I worked in Theoretical Computer Science and Machine Learning theory at [Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) under [Neeraj Kayal](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/neeraka/publications/) and [Ankit Garg](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/garga/); and in Post-quantum Cryptography with [Prof. Mahavir Jhanwar](https://sites.google.com/site/homeofmahavir/Home) at [Ashoka Mphasis Lab](https://mphasislab.ashoka.edu.in/).

I am actively seeking graduate programs in Math and Computer Science, as well as relevant industry positions. Please feel free to get in touch via [email](mailto:pritamchandra18@gmail.com).

I am a mathematician working mainly in matrix analysis, with a secondary interest in post-quantum cryptography and the theory of machine learning. I did my undergraduate and graduate work at Ashoka University, where I now continue as an independent researcher under the guidance of {% if page.bhatia_link %}[Rajendra Bhatia]({{ page.bhatia_link }}){% else %}Rajendra Bhatia{% endif %}, investigating inequalities between different means of positive definite matrices &mdash; at the level of operator orders, eigenvalues, and norms.

Between 2022 and 2024 I was a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, working with Neeraj Kayal, Ankit Garg, and Navin Goyal on questions in unsupervised learning and the theory of neural networks. More recently I have been studying lattice-based post-quantum cryptography, with a focus on the GPV framework and the Falcon signature scheme, curating expository notes meant to be readable with only elementary linear algebra.

This page collects some of that work, along with my teaching and academic service. For the everyday kind of writing &mdash; notes on books, films, songs, and other things &mdash; see the [blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}).

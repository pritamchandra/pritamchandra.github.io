<p class="verse">death has arrived
at my door again
ninth day in a row.
it reaches out
in whispers
to snap once and for all
the frail fiber
on which I am hung
to the edge of life.
this time I will go to bed
without hope in my heart
knowing full well
that this pain will not drop.
this pain will not drop
for here it is.
its cure lost
from memory
and from theory.
the pride of ruin appears
on the foaming mouth. <br>
death has arrived
at my door again
tenth day in a row.
I think I'll go.</p>

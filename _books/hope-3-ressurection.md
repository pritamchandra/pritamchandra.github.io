<p class = "verse">
God clothes with beauty
even the things of decay &mdash;
rusted poles. Moss-grown tombs. The tree
that will die
this sunset,
and our long cherished love.

At the distance the discarded high-rises
ruefully stick out
with a striking appeal.
Beauty is never fleeting anything.
It is only arriving more 
and more.

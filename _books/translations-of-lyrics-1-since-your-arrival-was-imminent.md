### Since your arrival was imminent

<p class="verse">Hearing your arrival is imminent
the rainclouds
have withheld the water
and the flame tree has held on
to her leaves.
The blind beggar is delaying his song,
and today, the cop at the intersection,
has let the bribes pass.

When the maestro found out
about your coming
he fumbled his rhythm,
and when the prime minister found out,
he made time to make love
to his wife.

Golden dreams are crowding my mind
For never before has a new day
peeped.
Now my questions are resisting
the lure of resolution,
and the nation is resisting plague,
all in the hopes of your coming.

And behold the forces of evil
are folding their paws,
and many mediums and conmen
are leaving their craft.
But young folks are restless still
for darkness gathers
in the far ends.
Yet, nearby, that young scoundrel,
on the verge of making trouble,
has hesitated
since your arrival is imminent.

Since your arrival is imminent,
I haven't sold my pen.</p>

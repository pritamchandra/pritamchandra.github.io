<p class="verse">
My sins curb my praise.
My lies have choked my throat.
My virtues are all vain.
My deeds are a show.

The lust of my heart
has cast me apart
and made me alone.

I'm drowning my fields
with whatever fills.
Fleeing always in haste,
I'm turning my gaze
to not meet the real.

I am dust without breath,
fear without faith.
I still gather my grit
to only flicker a bit
like a fire without flame.

I have lost my name,
my hope
and my home.
But the word just came ––
there is a death without death
that bears life, 
again.


Though stranded but a seed.
Though bruised but still a reed.
My breaking will be my rest.
On this word of the Best
a kernel I wait
to fall
and be blest.
</p>

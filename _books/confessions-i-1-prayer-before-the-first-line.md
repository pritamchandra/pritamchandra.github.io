<p class="verse">Let me start honest, or not at all &mdash;
no clean confession dressed as praise,
no doubt rehearsed until it flatters.
If I am going to speak to You
let it cost me something I can name.

I have written around this for years:
the shape of a prayer, never the prayer.
So here is the shape, emptied out.
Fill it or don&rsquo;t. I am done deciding
which one would hurt less.</p>

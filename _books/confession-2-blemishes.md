<p class="verse">
I see blemishes 
everywhere
although my friend's teethy giggles
are second to no insistence
for hanging on  . . . 
the same friend 
couldn't hold back
those smug sentences
dipped
in blemishes

smooth, electric touch of fingertips
are loving and assuring everything they trace 
on my skin
nails dig for my being
I long 
for lips
but my mouth touches a dry patch 
of winter mouth 
with cracks 
and blemishes

I got blemishes 
in every joy ––
the arrest of awareness
and a blemish of pride.
in each deed
my hand extends what looks like grace
the other slips down
goes within
trades pleasure
for blemishes

then, devastation of covers and sheets,
years and years
of hops, scratches, spins.
paws that are really a tautology 
for all that breathes 
but today
the old dog 
tiredly and quietly stares
at the high couch
I am struck again
with blemishes.
</p>

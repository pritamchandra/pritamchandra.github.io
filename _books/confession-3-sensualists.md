<p class="verse">
what is this sticky canvas
you are smearing my life on  . . . 
sticky bed this morning
sticky 
palms and phones.
sticky binges pick my meninges,
glue flows 
in its folds.

my deep side is glued to deep strife,
sticky thoughts
keep my sleep.
one careless fall
and neither truth nor awareness
can unstick
the sticky antics of flesh ––
barring my will,
my flesh sticks to all.

I see in the corner unseen, scattered 
in the dust
the sticky mousetrap  . . .  and a mouse,
no one knows how long it tried,
like a disjoint union of chunks
it lies
limbs torn apart,
but still
sticking to the crust.

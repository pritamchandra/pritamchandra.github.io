<p class = "verse">
Am I fine,
or am I not?
Everyday something is dying.
As I speak of this
some trace of thoughts
come and pass.
I can press on. I wish decay 
was not the rule
of the universe. Sometimes 
it's hard to believe
that it is all due to 
Adam's sin. My neck pain
and my shrinking lungs, 
my vigorous greed to be desired,
just because of Adam?

&ldquo;My this, my that&rdquo;
all the time
&hellip; come, let us fend for ourselves
for perhaps God won't &hellip; 
Adam sins to this day
and all things groan.
</p>

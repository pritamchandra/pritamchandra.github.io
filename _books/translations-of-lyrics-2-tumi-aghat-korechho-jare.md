*[Listen to Tumi Aghat Korechho Jare](https://youtu.be/g2Jx5pn3WEs)*

A glossary for the Bengali, before the original and the translation:

- Poritrata &mdash; purifier
- Tonu &mdash; body
- Rudhir &mdash; blood
- Pinko &mdash; mud
- Sholil &mdash; water
- Kallheri &mdash; calvary
- Shir &mdash; head
- Toron &mdash; gateway
- Onutap &mdash; repentance
- Deenota &mdash; poverty
- Jeerno &mdash; decayed

### Original

<p class="verse">Tumi aghath korecho jare &ndash; she je tomarei muktidata
Tumi apoman koro jare &ndash; se je tomarei poritrata

Oi shundor tonu rudhir dharay bhashe
Jeno shukhe komol ponko sholile hashe

Kalbheri shire shononi ki tumi
moha jiboner gatha

Tumi aghath korecho jare &ndash; she je tomarei muktidata
Tumi apoman koro jare &ndash; se je tomarei poritrata

Mrittu toron paar hote Jodi chaav
Krusher barta he pothik shune java

Onutap loye bolo shudhu ekbar
&ldquo;Khoma koro probhu deenota khoma aamar&rdquo;

Jeerno jibone pushpito hobe
prem boshonto lota

Tumi aghath korecho jare &ndash; she je tomarei muktidata
Tumi apoman koro jare &ndash; se je tomarei poritrata</p>

<p class="verse">oh live wire, quiet fire
and passing train
be mine
be mine

tired liver, oh deep river
falling bridge and leaking boat
be mine
be mine

frozen brain and chronic pain
dark alleys and raging blades
be mine
be mine

if I were a little brave
I could myself be mine
but you dear friend
be mine
be mine</p>

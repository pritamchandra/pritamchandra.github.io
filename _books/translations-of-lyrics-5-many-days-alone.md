### Many days alone

<p class="verse">The carnival that you hosted
&emsp;was where I first saw a radio
and I just couldn't get over it.
&emsp;Your house was so warm
towards the left side
&emsp;of the little lake.

But I kept thinking
about this radio ever since I saw it.
&emsp;It utterly became my obsession
&emsp;&emsp;and the obsession
has made me dull
&emsp;and has even
stunted my growth.
&emsp;Even today it's on my mind!

Later in the carnival
&emsp;I was aimlessly wandering &mdash;
&emsp;so many colors, so many sounds,
so many faces
&emsp;overwhelmed my hungry brain.
It was there I first met your eyes
&emsp;&emsp;and what eyes I met!
&emsp;I swear nobody else
can pull off the blink that
&emsp;you had blinked.
&emsp;&emsp;Nobody!

&emsp;Then one day just like that
I gave up my all to you
&emsp;and at the same moment somehow
borrowed from you
&emsp;&emsp;this life's load of debt.

But that's all an aside.
&emsp;It's the radio that I saw
&emsp;&emsp;in the carnival,
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;in your unsually warm house.
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;I just can't get over it.</p>
